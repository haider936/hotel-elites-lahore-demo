A simple, well-located stay in Gulberg

Hotel Elites sits on M.M. Alam Road — the Gulberg strip known for its cafés, restaurants and shops. It is a practical budget base for business trips, family visits and shopping trips to Lahore, with Liberty Market close by.

Staying in Gulberg III puts you in the middle of the city's everyday life: Liberty Market's shops and food are a short walk away, and the cafés and restaurants of M.M. Alam Road are right on the doorstep.

Rooms are air-conditioned and come with a TV — the essentials for a comfortable night's rest after a day out in Lahore. To check availability and book, call the hotel directly on +92 42 35760064 or +92 42 35760065.
